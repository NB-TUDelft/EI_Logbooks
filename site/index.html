How it works

  1. Open your logbook. Your work is saved in this browser as you type, and is there again when you come back.
  2. Fill it in. Type in the boxes and use Insert image for graphs and photos. Image page and Text page add a page at the end.
  3. Export PDF when you are done, and hand in that file on Brightspace.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Nothing is uploaded: your answers stay on your own computer until you hand in the PDF. The shield button in the logbook deletes what this browser kept.
