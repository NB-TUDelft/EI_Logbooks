NB2420 Electronic Instrumentation: logbooks

Open your logbook, fill it in, add photos or plots, and save it as a PDF to hand in on BrightSpace. Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

  1. Open a logbook below. Your work is kept in this browser while you go.
  2. Type in the boxes. Use Insert image in the graph box, or the toolbar to add text, drawings, images and extra pages.
  3. Press Ctrl+S (or the save button) to save the PDF, and hand in that file.
  4. To keep working on a saved PDF later, open it here with the folder button or drag it onto the page.
